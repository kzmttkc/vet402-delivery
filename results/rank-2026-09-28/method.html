How we measure

Method v2 · report 2026-09-28 · change log

1. Who pays

vet402 buys with its own funds and takes no money from sellers: no listing fee, no paid placement, no referral cut. Call counts, payer counts and anything a seller reports about itself are not inputs, so a seller cannot buy a better place by paying itself (wash trading).

2. What "arrived" means

Delivered = vet402's payment settled, then the seller answered 2xx with a non-empty body. The same test on every chain.

Separate column, never part of the grade: whether the answer's keys or shape matched what the seller declared. Checked only where the runner compared them (Algorand census, Solana census) and the seller declared something.

3. Who was at fault

Counted = delivered purchases plus failures on the seller's side (payment settled and nothing usable came back, or the seller's server answered 5xx).

Not counted, shown as numbers: failures on vet402's or the facilitator's side, failures where the cause can't be told, endpoints that were not buyable, vet402's own skips (price cap, input it will not make up, duplicates) and refusals because payTo changed.

A failed purchase gets the first rule that matches, top to bottom.

  1. 1. seller side · paid_not_delivered · this run: 33
    vet402's payment settled, then the seller answered non-2xx or an empty body.
  2. 2. vet402 or facilitator side · rate_limited_429 · this run: 605
    HTTP 429. vet402 bought many items from one seller within minutes; one ordinary purchase would not hit the limit.
  3. 3. vet402 or facilitator side · subcent_quota · this run: 363
    subcent_quota_exceeded: vet402's many sub-cent purchases used up the per-payer quota.
  4. 4. vet402 or facilitator side · payment_tx_rejected · this run: 18
    The payment transaction vet402 built was rejected before settling: simulation failed, BlockhashNotFound, already in ledger, validity window passed, facilitator unavailable.
  5. 5. seller side · server_error_5xx · this run: 49
    The seller's server answered 5xx and no settlement happened.
  6. 6. can't tell · timeout · this run: 19
    vet402's request timed out. A slow seller and a short vet402 timeout look the same.
  7. 7. can't tell · request_rejected_4xx · this run: 153
    The seller answered 4xx (400, 401, 404, 422 …) before any payment settled. vet402 made the input, so the cause cannot be told apart.
  8. 8. can't tell · payment_not_accepted_402 · this run: 43
    The seller answered 402 again after vet402 paid, with no reason that points to either side.
  9. 9. can't tell · answer_without_settlement · this run: 13
    The seller answered 2xx but no settlement of vet402's payment could be found.
  10. 10. can't tell · unclassified · this run: 0
    None of the above. Counted here so a new failure mode is visible and never lowers a grade.

4. Grades and rank numbers

95% Wilson score interval of delivered / counted (z = 1.96). A: lower bound ≥ 0.9. B: lower bound ≥ 0.75. C: lower bound ≥ 0.5. D: upper bound < 0.5. Otherwise undecided. Even 10 deliveries out of 10 have a lower bound of 0.72 (grade C); an A needs about 35 out of 35.

A rank number needs at least 10 counted purchases on at least 2 different UTC days. Below that the seller is listed as "measuring", with no grade and no number.

Sellers with a rank number: lower bound desc, then delivered rate desc, then counted purchases desc, then seller name. Equal values share a number. Measuring sellers follow by name.

This report: A 6 · B 14 · C 2 · D 0 · ? 1 · … 271

5. How we buy

From method v2 on: at most 5 purchases per seller in one run, at least 60 s apart. Amounts, payTo checks and money caps are unchanged. The 2026-09-27/28 Algorand runs predate this and bought up to ~500 items of one seller in under an hour.

Seller = hostname. When one host fronts several catalog services that each pay their own recipient (a proxy), each service is its own seller: host#service. payTo changed = the same chain and URL asked for a different recipient in a later run, or vet402 refused to pay because the recipient differed from the one it had recorded. One recipient per chain is not a change. Shown as a flag; it does not change the grade.

7. Mistakes and corrections

A seller who thinks a row is wrong opens a GitHub issue with the URL or tx. vet402 checks it against the chain and the raw result; a wrong row is fixed and the fix is noted in the change log.

Open an issue on GitHub · each seller page has a prefilled link.

8. Limits

  • A seller that recognises vet402's payer address could serve vet402 better than others. The payer addresses are public.
  • Purchases from one seller in the same run are not independent (one outage fails many at once), so the interval is narrower than it should be for runs before v2 pacing.
  • The Tempo runner kept no response body, so on Tempo 'delivered' means settled + 2xx without the body test.
  • The vet402-or-facilitator rules for 429 and subcent_quota_exceeded rest on how vet402 bought, not on the seller's word. If paced purchases still get them, they move to the seller side.
  • Some inputs are local files of vet402's runners; their sha256 is recorded but the files are not yet published.

Change log

  • v2 (2026-09-28)
    • Failures are split by who was at fault: seller side, vet402 or facilitator side, can't tell. Only seller-side failures count toward the grade; the other two are shown as counts.
    • HTTP 429 and subcent_quota_exceeded from vet402 buying hundreds of one seller's items within minutes are vet402's side.
    • One delivery test on every chain: settled, then 2xx with a non-empty body. Whether the answer matched what the seller declared is a separate column.
    • Rank numbers only with 10+ counted purchases on 2+ different days; everyone else is "measuring".
    • Grades A–D. Good marks by the interval's lower bound; D only when the upper bound is below 0.5.
    • Measuring from now on: at most 5 purchases per seller per run, 60 s apart.
  • v1 (2026-09-28)
    • Score = Wilson lower bound of delivered / every tried purchase. Each chain runner's own delivery check. Every seller with one try got a rank number.
