Robinhood Chain: paid in USDG, checked against the feed

Robinhood's developer docs ask apps that read Stock Token prices to "reject stale prices" and, when mixing a share price with a token price, to "apply currentMultiplier". vet402 buys stock prices that x402 sellers offer on Robinhood Chain, pays in USDG, and holds each answer to those checks against the Chainlink feed of that Stock Token.

The daily record on Robinhood Chain

After each UTC day with purchases, vet402 writes one transaction on Robinhood Chain from its wallet to itself. Its input data is the text vet402-purchases/v0 chain=eip155:4663 day=… root=… n=…: the Merkle root of that day's purchase records (each leaf is keccak256 of one record's canonical JSON). Anyone holding a record can recompute its leaf and check it against the root on chain.

How vet402 pays for this

vet402 pays from one wallet, 0x9B59…4E51, the same on every EVM chain it buys on, with the seller's own x402 402: exact scheme, EIP-3009 transferWithAuthorization only (no Permit2, no approve), to the payTo the 402 named when vet402 planned the purchase, never more than that price. On this chain: at most 0.10 USDG a purchase, 1.00 USDG in total and 20 purchases, in a ledger of its own. The facilitator the seller chose submits the payment and pays its gas.
