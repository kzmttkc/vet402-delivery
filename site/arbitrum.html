The daily record on Arbitrum One

After each UTC day with purchases, vet402 writes one transaction on Arbitrum One from its wallet to itself. Its input data is the text vet402-purchases/v0 chain=eip155:42161 day=… root=… n=…: the Merkle root of that day's purchase records (each leaf is keccak256 of one record's canonical JSON). Anyone holding a record can recompute its leaf and check it against the root on chain.

The same root can go to a contract, DeliveryRoots.sol, whose verify(day, digest, proof) lets another contract ask in one call whether vet402 recorded a purchase. It is tested, not deployed yet.

How vet402 pays for this

vet402 pays from one wallet, 0x9B59…4E51, the same on every EVM chain it buys on, with the seller's own x402 402: exact scheme, EIP-3009 transferWithAuthorization only (no Permit2, no approve), to the payTo the 402 named when vet402 planned the purchase, never more than that price. On this chain: at most 0.10 USDC a purchase, 2.00 USDC in total and 90 purchases, in a ledger of its own. The facilitator the seller chose submits the payment and pays its gas.
